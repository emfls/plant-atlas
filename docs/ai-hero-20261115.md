## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic, softly lit botanical learning atmosphere beside a window: an open blank ivory notebook with no writing or drawings, a simple metal hand lens and a plain pencil arranged on pale linen and a light oak desk. Warm ivory paper, pale sage green, gentle natural daylight, subtle leaf shadows, real linen fibers and wood texture, refined editorial still-life photography. Wide composition that center-crops elegantly for a website hero. No person, no hands, no scientific illustration, no labeled specimen, no identification chart, no herbarium sample, no edible or medicinal claim, no text, no handwriting, no labels, no brand, no logo, no watermark, no collage or borders. This image is only an AI-staged mood for learning about plants, not a species photograph or identification reference.
